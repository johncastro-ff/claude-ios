## 2. Core Campaign Strategies

### Strategy 1: The Treatment Spotlight Campaign

**Objective:** Drive consultations for a specific high-ticket treatment
**Best For:** Flagship devices, new equipment launches, seasonal treatments

**Structure:**
- **Top of Funnel (TOF):** Educational video or carousel explaining what the treatment does, who it's for, and what to expect
- **Middle of Funnel (MOF):** Patient testimonial or provider walkthrough showing the actual experience
- **Bottom of Funnel (BOF):** Direct offer — consultation booking with urgency element

**Creative Mix:**
- 1x educational Reel/video (60-90 sec)
- 1x provider "meet the expert" video (30-45 sec)
- 2x static image ads (treatment spotlight + offer)
- 1x carousel (treatment process walkthrough)

**Targeting:**
- Interest-based: skincare, aesthetic treatments, specific competitor brands
- Lookalike: based on past consultation bookers or high-value patients
- Geo: 15–30 mile radius of the practice (adjust for metro density)

---

### Strategy 2: The Authority Builder

**Objective:** Position the med spa as the #1 expert in their market
**Best For:** Practices with experienced providers, multiple advanced devices, or board-certified staff

**Structure:**
- **Content Pillars:** Myth-busting, treatment comparisons, "what your provider should tell you" education
- **Format:** Short-form video, carousel infographics, provider Q&A
- **CTA:** Soft — "Follow for more" or "Save this for later" (with retargeting to capture engaged users)

**Why It Works:**
- Builds the retargeting pool with high-intent audiences
- Reduces cost per consultation when BOF ads hit warm audiences
- Creates organic sharing that amplifies paid reach

### Strategy 3: The Seasonal Surge

**Objective:** Capitalize on seasonal demand windows with time-bound offers
**Best For:** Driving volume during peak treatment seasons

**Seasonal Calendar:**

| Season | Window | Treatments | Angle |
|--------|--------|------------|-------|
| **New Year** | Jan 1–31 | Body contouring, skin rejuvenation | "New year, new confidence" — resolution-driven |
| **Spring Prep** | Mar–Apr | Laser treatments, injectables | "Get ready for summer" — preparation narrative |
| **Summer Glow** | May–Jun | HydraFacial, BBL, light treatments | "Your summer skin starts now" — urgency before events |
| **Wedding Season** | Apr–Jun | Full-face packages, body sculpting | "Look your best on the big day" — event-driven |
| **Back to School** | Aug–Sep | Acne treatments, skin refresh | Parent self-care angle — "your turn now" |
| **Holiday Glow** | Oct–Dec | Package deals, gift cards, full rejuvenation | "Give the gift of confidence" — gifting + self-treat |
| **Tax Refund** | Feb–Apr | High-ticket device treatments | "Invest in yourself" — financial framing |

**Offer Structures:**
- Package discounts (buy 3 sessions, get 1 free)
- Complimentary add-on with flagship treatment
- Financing highlight (0% APR through CareCredit/PatientFi)
- Limited consultation slots (scarcity)

### Strategy 4: The New Patient Acquisition Funnel

**Objective:** Systematically convert cold audiences into booked consultations
**Best For:** Practices looking for predictable, scalable patient flow

**Funnel Architecture:**

```
Cold Audience (Interest/Lookalike)
        │
        ▼
   [Educational Ad]  ──→  Watch 50%+?  ──→  [Retarget: Offer Ad]
        │                                          │
        ▼                                          ▼
   [Engagement Ad]   ──→  Engaged?    ──→  [Retarget: Testimonial]
                                                   │
                                                   ▼
                                          [Retarget: Consultation CTA]
                                                   │
                                                   ▼
                                          [Landing Page / Booking]
```

**Key Metrics to Target:**
- Cost per lead (consultation booked): $30–$80
- Cost per show (patient arrives): $60–$150
- Cost per conversion (treatment purchased): $150–$400
- ROAS target: 4x–8x within 90 days (varies by treatment value)

### Strategy 5: The Equipment ROI Maximizer

**Objective:** Fill appointment slots for a specific device to accelerate equipment ROI
**Best For:** Practices that just purchased expensive new equipment

**The Problem:** A $150K device sitting idle costs the practice roughly $400/day in depreciation alone. Every empty slot compounds the loss.

**The Approach:**
1. **Launch week:** Announcement campaign — "Now offering [Treatment] with [Device]"
2. **Weeks 2-4:** Education blitz — what the device does, why it's different, clinical results
3. **Ongoing:** Steady-state acquisition campaigns with proven creative
4. **Monthly:** Refresh creative with new testimonials and seasonal angles

**Pricing Psychology for High-Ticket Devices:**
- Lead with per-session value, then show package savings
- Anchor against surgical alternatives ("Achieve similar results to [surgery] without downtime — starting at $X per session")
- Always include financing options in ad copy
- Show "investment" framing, not "cost"

## 3. Ad Creative Tactics

### Tactic 1: The Hook Formula

The first 3 seconds determine everything. Use these proven hook structures:

| Hook Type | Example | Best For |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Problem-Agitate** | "Tired of stubborn fat that won't go away no matter how much you diet?" | Body contouring |
| **Myth Buster** | "You do NOT need surgery to tighten loose skin. Here's why..." | Non-surgical treatments |
| **Social Proof** | "Over 200 patients have transformed their skin with us this year" | Established practices |
| **Curiosity Gap** | "The treatment celebrities are getting — and your med spa probably offers it" | Trending devices |
| **Direct Offer** | "This month only: $500 off Morpheus8 packages at [Practice]" | Seasonal campaigns |
| **Provider Authority** | "As a board-certified dermatologist, here's what I recommend for..." | Provider-led content |

### Tactic 2: The Offer Stack

Never lead with just a discount. Stack value:

**Example: CoolSculpting Campaign**
```
✦ $500 OFF CoolSculpting Elite packages (3+ areas)
✦ FREE body composition analysis ($150 value)
✦ Complimentary post-treatment lymphatic session
✦ 0% financing available through CareCredit
✦ Only 8 slots available this month

→ Book Your Free Consultation Today
```

### Tactic 3: The Comparison Framework

Position treatments against alternatives (without disparaging):

- **Non-surgical vs. surgical:** "Achieve visible contouring without surgery, anesthesia, or downtime"
- **Professional vs. at-home:** "Clinical-grade treatments deliver results that at-home devices simply can't match"
- **This device vs. older technology:** "Next-generation RF microneedling — deeper penetration, faster results, less discomfort"

### Tactic 4: The Education-to-Conversion Bridge

**Step 1:** Teach something genuinely useful (builds trust)
**Step 2:** Connect the lesson to a treatment (builds desire)
**Step 3:** Make the next step easy (books consultation)

**Example:**
> "Most people don't know that collagen production drops 1% every year after age 25. That's why skin loses firmness and fine lines appear. Morpheus8 uses RF microneedling to trigger your body's natural collagen response — essentially resetting the clock. Want to see if it's right for you? [Practice] is offering complimentary consultations this month."

### Tactic 5: The Social Proof Engine

- **Video testimonials** outperform static quotes 3-5x on Meta
- Ask patients: "What almost stopped you from booking?" (addresses objections)
- Show the *journey*, not just the result: nervous arrival → comfortable treatment → confident follow-up
- Always get written consent (HIPAA compliance)
- Never promise the viewer will get the same results

## 4. Targeting & Audience Architecture

### Core Audiences

| Audience | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| **Interest Stack** | Cold | Skincare, dermatology, cosmetic procedures, specific brands (Botox, CoolSculpting, etc.) |
| **Demographic Layer** | Cold | Women 28-62, HHI $75K+, homeowners (proxy for disposable income) |
| **Lookalike - Patients** | Warm | 1-3% lookalike based on patient email list |
| **Lookalike - Consultations** | Warm | 1-3% lookalike based on consultation bookers |
| **Engaged - Video** | Warm | 50%+ video viewers (last 30-90 days) |
| **Engaged - Social** | Warm | Page/profile engagers (last 30-90 days) |
| **Website Visitors** | Hot | All visitors last 30 days, or treatment-page specific visitors |
| **Abandoned Booking** | Hot | Started but didn't complete consultation booking |

### Geo-Targeting Rules

- **Suburban/rural practices:** 25-35 mile radius
- **Urban/metro practices:** 10-20 mile radius
- **Luxury positioning:** Overlay with affluent ZIP codes
- **Multi-location:** Separate campaigns per location, 15-mile radius each

### Exclusion Lists (Critical)

- Existing patients (unless running upsell/cross-sell)
- Under 18 (Meta policy + treatment appropriateness)
- Recent converters (30-day window to prevent ad fatigue)
- Competitors' staff (optional, but smart to exclude known competitor pages)

## 5. Landing Page & Conversion Optimization

### The High-Ticket Consultation Landing Page

**Required Elements:**
1. **Headline:** Treatment-specific, benefit-driven ("Sculpt Your Ideal Body — No Surgery Required")
2. **Hero image/video:** The treatment experience or device in action
3. **3-4 key benefits:** Icon + short text (quick scan format)
4. **Social proof:** 2-3 testimonials with photos
5. **Provider credentials:** Board certifications, years of experience, training
6. **The offer:** Clear, specific, time-bound
7. **Booking widget:** Embedded directly on page (NEVER send to a separate site)
8. **FAQ section:** Address top 5 objections
9. **Trust badges:** Device manufacturer certified, medical associations, review scores

### Conversion Rate Benchmarks

- Landing page → consultation booked: 8-15%
- Consultation booked → consultation attended: 60-75%
- Consultation attended → treatment purchased: 40-60%
- **Target: $30-80 cost per consultation booked**

## 7. Reporting & KPIs

### Weekly Dashboard Metrics

- **Spend:** Total + daily average
- **Impressions & Reach:** Are we reaching enough people?
- **CPM:** Cost efficiency of delivery
- **CTR:** Ad creative effectiveness (target: 1.5%+ for cold, 3%+ for retargeting)
- **Cost per Lead:** Consultation bookings
- **Lead Quality Score:** % that show up, % that convert
- **ROAS:** Revenue attributed to ad spend

### Monthly Performance Review

- Creative performance ranking (retire bottom 20%, scale top 20%)
- Audience performance comparison
- Funnel conversion rates at each stage
- Competitive landscape shifts
- Recommendations for next month's strategy

## 8. Compliance Checklist

### Before Every Campaign Launch

- [ ] No before/after images that guarantee results
- [ ] No language that implies certainty of outcomes ("will," "guaranteed," "permanent")
- [ ] No targeting based on health conditions or personal attributes
- [ ] All testimonials have signed consent/release forms
- [ ] Pricing is accurate and clearly disclosed
- [ ] "Results may vary" disclaimer included where applicable
- [ ] Landing page includes practice credentials and licensing
- [ ] No use of trademarked device names in ways that violate manufacturer agreements
- [ ] Financing terms include required legal disclosures
- [ ] All patient images/videos have HIPAA-compliant release forms

### Launch Day

- [ ] Campaigns built and reviewed
- [ ] Ad copy proofread (pricing, dates, phone numbers)
- [ ] Creative assets uploaded at correct specs
- [ ] Targeting verified (geo, audiences, exclusions)
- [ ] Budget set correctly (daily, not lifetime, for initial testing)
- [ ] Ads submitted for Meta review
- [ ] Client notified of launch

### Post-Launch (First 7 Days)

- [ ] Check delivery daily — are ads spending?
- [ ] Monitor for Meta rejections (appeal if policy-compliant)
- [ ] Track early CPM/CTR signals
- [ ] No major optimizations yet (let the learning phase complete)
- [ ] Respond to ad comments/messages within 4 hours
- [ ] Day 7 performance snapshot to client
