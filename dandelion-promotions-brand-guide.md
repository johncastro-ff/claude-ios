## Tone of Voice

### Core Principles

1. **Confident, Not Arrogant** — We know what works. We let results speak.
2. **Clinical Precision, Human Warmth** — Data-driven language with empathetic understanding of the patient journey.
3. **Premium Without Pretension** — Luxury positioning that stays approachable and clear.
4. **Action-Oriented** — Every piece of copy drives toward a next step.

### Voice Examples

| Context | ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Describing services | "We engineer Meta ad campaigns that fill your treatment calendar with qualified, high-ticket patients." | "We do social media marketing for med spas." |
| Urgency | "Limited availability — 3 onboarding slots remaining this month." | "HURRY! SALE ENDS SOON!!!" |
| Results | "Our clients average a 4.2x ROAS within 90 days." | "We get amazing results for everyone!" |
| CTA | "Book your strategy session" | "Click here to learn more" |

### Vocabulary Guide

**Use:** treatments, patients, practice, aesthetic, advanced, precision, transform, results, high-ticket, qualified leads, ROAS, treatment calendar, consultation pipeline

**Avoid:** cheap, discount, basic, customers (use "patients"), buy (use "book" or "schedule"), tricks, hacks, viral

## Visual Guidelines

### Photography Style

- **Treatment imagery:** Clean, clinical environments with warm lighting. Show the equipment and the experience, not just results.
- **Before/After:** MUST comply with Meta's advertising policies. Use "transformation" language. Never guarantee outcomes.
- **Lifestyle:** Aspirational but attainable. Confident individuals, natural settings. Diverse representation.
- **Equipment:** Hero shots of advanced devices (Morpheus8, CoolSculpting Elite, Stellar M22, etc.) — positions the med spa as cutting-edge.

### Composition Rules

- **Clean negative space** — Luxury breathes. Never crowd a layout.
- **Asymmetric balance** — Avoid rigid centering. Offset imagery with text for visual interest.
- **Gold accents sparingly** — Dandelion Gold is the punctuation, not the paragraph.
- **Dark mode default** — All agency materials default to dark backgrounds (Obsidian/Charcoal) for premium feel.

### Ad Creative Templates

#### Format 1: Single Treatment Spotlight
- Dark background
- Hero treatment/equipment image (60% of frame)
- Treatment name in Playfair Display
- 2-3 benefit bullets in Inter
- Price anchor or financing CTA
- Dandelion Gold accent line or button

#### Format 2: Offer Stack
- Split layout (image left, copy right)
- Lead with the primary offer (e.g., "$500 off CoolSculpting packages")
- Stack 2-3 bonus incentives below
- Urgency element (limited slots, seasonal window)
- Clear "Book Consultation" CTA

#### Format 3: Social Proof
- Patient transformation story (compliant framing)
- Quote or testimonial overlay
- Treatment details in secondary text
- Practice logo and trust badges
- CTA to book same treatment

#### Format 4: Authority/Education
- "Did you know?" or myth-busting hook
- Clean infographic or stat presentation
- Position the med spa as the expert
- Soft CTA to learn more or schedule consultation

## Logo Usage

### Primary Mark
- **Dandelion icon** (stylized dandelion seed head) + **"Dandelion Promotions"** wordmark
- Minimum clear space: 1x the height of the icon on all sides
- Minimum size: 120px wide for digital, 1 inch for print

### Color Variations
- **Primary:** Dandelion Gold icon + Pearl wordmark on Obsidian background
- **Reversed:** Obsidian icon + Obsidian wordmark on Pearl/light background
- **Monochrome:** All Pearl (on dark) or all Obsidian (on light)

### Restrictions
- Never stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
- Never place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Never change the brand colors to match a client's palette

## Med Spa Advertising Compliance Notes

### Meta Advertising Policies (Critical)

- **No before/after comparisons** that imply guaranteed results
- **No body shaming** or negative self-image language
- **No targeting** based on health conditions
- **No misleading claims** about treatment outcomes
- Use language like "results may vary" and "individual results depend on multiple factors"
- Focus on the *experience* and *technology* rather than promised outcomes

### HIPAA Considerations
- Never use patient data without explicit written consent
- Testimonials require signed release forms
- Remove all identifying information from analytics shared externally

### FTC Guidelines
- All testimonials must reflect genuine experiences
- Disclose any material connections or incentives
- Avoid superlatives without substantiation ("best," "most advanced," "#1")
